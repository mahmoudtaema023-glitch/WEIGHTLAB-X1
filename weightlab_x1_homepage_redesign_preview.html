03 / Engineering principles

Built to explore, not to fake certainty.

WEIGHTLAB X1 separates visual concepts, mathematical models, and measured prototype results so that each can be understood on its own terms.

Simulation is not a physical test

Models use stated assumptions and simplified relationships. Real components can behave differently because of friction, tolerances, temperature, noise, material properties, and assembly.

Calibration is evidence

Accuracy claims require actual reference measurements, a documented method, repeat trials, and an honest account of uncertainty.

Project integrity: The website is an educational design and simulation platform. Its 3D shapes and calculated outputs are not certified engineering drawings, validated finite-element analysis, or proof of real-world scale accuracy.
