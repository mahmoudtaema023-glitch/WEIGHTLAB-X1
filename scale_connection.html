Connection

One device at a time

Use a data-capable USB cable. Your scale must expose a serial port; close Arduino Serial Monitor before connecting.

Advanced serial settings

Connect the laptop and scale to the same network. Enter the endpoint provided by your firmware or local bridge.

An HTTPS website needs WSS or HTTPS with a trusted certificate. Plain ws:// or http:// device endpoints require a locally served HTTP copy. HTTP polling also needs the device to allow your website origin with CORS.

Bluetooth LE requires matching GATT identifiers. Bluetooth Classic is not BLE; an OS-paired serial device may work through the USB serial tab.

UUIDs accept a full 128-bit value or 0x181d-style short identifiers. The measurement characteristic must notify or indicate.

Reading format

Examples: 123.45 g or {"weight":123.45,"unit":"g","stable":true}. USB readings must end with a newline. Standard BLE weight measurements use their own binary unit flags.

A successful new connection or demo starts a fresh session. Export existing readings first. Supported measurements: ±1 trillion grams.

HARDWARE / COMPATIBILITY

A shared interface. Different boards.

Choose firmware that sends calibrated weight readings in the supported format. A connection cannot add a radio that your board does not have or interpret an unknown proprietary protocol.

ESP32 family

Use USB serial plus Wi-Fi and BLE where your exact chip supports them. Not every ESP32 variant has both wireless radios. Configure a text-notification service or a secure network endpoint.

Arduino boards

Classic Uno/Nano boards can send newline-delimited measurements over USB serial. Wi-Fi and BLE require compatible add-on modules or a board with those radios built in.

Raspberry Pi & other scales

A Pi can expose a network endpoint or act as a bridge. USB gadget and BLE peripheral modes need specific configuration. Proprietary USB HID, binary, or vendor protocols require a matching adapter.

Before you connect

Use a current Chrome or Edge on your laptop and check the capability banner above. Bluetooth support depends on your OS and adapter; Linux support can differ. Open the actual website in a normal browser tab: an embedded preview may block hardware permissions.

USB: match baud/settings, select the scale in the browser chooser, and send one reading per line. BLE: use the measurement service and characteristic from your firmware. Wi-Fi: use your device's network endpoint, not the public website URL; HTTPS pages require trusted TLS, and HTTP polling requires CORS. Browser local-network permission may also be needed.

Read the full firmware and setup guide for hardware-specific notes. These are software adapters, not proof of scale accuracy or certification.
