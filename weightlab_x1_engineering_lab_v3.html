MODEL NOTES

What the model assumes

The calculation uses F = m × g and x = F ÷ k.

  • Gravity is approximated as 9.81 m/s².
  • The spring is assumed linear and ideal.
  • Friction, damping, fatigue, tilt, and material limits are not simulated.
  • Real springs need measured stiffness and safe operating limits.
The displayed deflection is a mathematical estimate, not a construction instruction or a guarantee that a real spring can safely support that load.
